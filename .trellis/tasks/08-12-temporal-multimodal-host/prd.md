# Temporal media and multimodal host

## Goal

Make time a first-class Design OS dimension and provide narrowly verified
multimodal Host capabilities for product, launch, brand, UI and director-led
media without turning Cutout into a short-drama tool or full NLE.

## Requirements

Delivery is split into two independently validated gates. Gate A is the minimum
competition-critical multimodal Host: exact text/image/video capability
contracts, required DashScope transports, verified downloads and media QA. Gate
B is the broader Temporal Design foundation: Media Timeline, non-destructive
editing, cross-profile fixtures and additional reviewed routes. The Competition
Host depends only on Gate A; Gate B cannot delay the benchmark package or weaken
Gate A's contracts.

- Preserve `motion-ir.v1` for deterministic UI/vector interaction motion. Add
  `media-timeline.v1` for sequences, scenes, shots, takes, source/edit ranges,
  tracks, transitions, audio roles, captions/overlays, variants, review markers
  and delivery versions. The two IRs may reference common Design materials but
  are not interchangeable.
- Represent product, logo/text, character, wardrobe, environment, palette,
  camera, lighting and audio identity as explicit continuity locks. New takes
  replace selected ranges non-destructively; unaffected regions retain exact
  revisions, hashes and lineage.
- Expose granular video operations for accepted reference modalities,
  text/image-to-video, first/last frame, extend, range edit/replace, transfer,
  lip/audio sync, native audio, multi-shot and background/control modes. A
  generic marketing label never grants capability.
- Separate portable capability contracts from Provider wire adapters. Every
  route is the intersection of declared model, observed/verified operation,
  accepted limits and executable Host transport, with truthful receipts.
- Extract reusable DashScope request/poll/download behavior behind native secret
  and origin custody. Add exact structured text, VL/OCR, image and asynchronous
  Wan video routes needed by commerce; validate downloaded bytes before success.
- Admit MiniMax H3, Seedance 2.5 or aggregator routes only after official wire
  documentation and live probes verify their exact operations. Unsupported
  features remain capability-required.
- Validate decode/playback, dimensions, duration, frame rate, safe area,
  caption/OCR, continuity, brand/text, audio presence/loudness and delivery
  format with timecode-bound evidence.
- Provide cross-profile temporal fixtures for UI/product walkthrough,
  brand/campaign composition and multi-shot directed media. Do not encode a
  short-drama grammar or build unrestricted compositing, grading or mixing.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] TA1: Exact structured-text, vision/OCR, image and Wan video routes needed
      by Commerce pass mock transport, origin, receipt and artifact validation.
- [ ] TA2: Asynchronous success exists only after allowlisted download bytes pass
      container, codec, playback, size and duration validation.
- [ ] TA3: Timeout, 408/429/5xx, Retry-After, cancellation, malformed task state,
      bad origin, oversized artifact and late callback tests fail safely without
      duplicate spend or stale publication.
- [ ] TB1: The three cross-profile fixtures use the same Timeline schema while
      Profile-owned labels and narrative roles remain removable.
- [ ] TB2: Replacing one time range creates a new take and leaves every
      unaffected region's revision, hash and lineage unchanged.
- [ ] TB3: H3-, Seedance- and Wan-shaped descriptors authorize only exact proven
      operations and limits; generic video/edit claims cannot route execution.
- [ ] TB4: Every receipt records actual accepted references, locks, operation,
      Provider/model, limits and output hash; unsupported routes stay unavailable.
- [ ] TB5: Timecode-bound continuity, brand/text, caption, safe-area and audio
      findings block invalid delivery and support targeted replacement.

## Out Of Scope

- Full Premiere/Resolve replacement, arbitrary frame compositing, plugin hosting,
  unrestricted codecs, grading, mixing or short-drama-specific story grammar.
- Claiming all Desktop/headless Hosts support video before their route proof.
- Treating 302.ai marketing metadata as universal capability truth.
