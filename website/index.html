01 / Outcome

One intent.
A complete visual system.

Cutout plans the right scope for the work in front of it. A restaurant, a game, and a desktop tool should not share the same route topology, Design System, or material plan. The Agent derives each from the actual domain and keeps the whole production graph connected.
